# Acknowledgement

As far as I know, the first to document conventions of this kind
was @snedashkovsky in [semantic conventions](https://github.com/Snedashkovsky/cyber-semantic-conventions).
He did this in July 2021, some months before the launch of Bostrom.
The examples ran on the test network.
The form itself probably started earlier,
as an implementation of the ideas of @mastercyb behind cyb.ai.
Sergey gave them a name and a description.
His document encodes application objects: tweet, follow, avatar.
The CID of a special string, such as tweet, is put in the source of an edge.
This CID marks the target as an object of that type.
This is the CID of the string itself, not of a record as in the appendix "Cybergraph format".
He also proposed a meta level:
cyberlinks from the semantic convention root declare a convention itself in the graph.
And the list of conventions is read from the graph itself.

And, of course, the whole Cybergraph model has many forms,
and this issue describes some of them. The whole model, in a much larger volume, started and lives in the minds of the founders @mastercyb and @cyborgshead.
Everything that this journal describes is only rediscovered ideas of the Bostrom founders, or ideas derived from them.
